# Data Card: COMPAS Recidivism Dataset

Following Gebru et al. (2021) "Datasheets for Datasets"

## Motivation

### Purpose

**Why was the dataset created?**
The COMPAS (Correctional Offender Management Profiling for Alternative Sanctions) dataset was compiled by ProPublica as part of their investigative journalism project examining racial bias in risk assessment tools used by the US criminal justice system [Angwin et al., 2016]. The original COMPAS risk scores were generated by Northpointe (now Equivant) as proprietary predictions of recidivism risk.

**Who created the dataset?**
- **Data collection:** Broward County Sheriff's Office and Broward County Clerk's Office (Florida)
- **Compilation and release:** ProPublica (Julia Angwin, Jeff Larson, Surya Mattu, Lauren Kirchner)
- **Date:** Published May 2016

**Funding:** ProPublica (nonprofit investigative newsroom)

## Composition

### Instance Description

**What do the instances represent?**
Each instance represents a defendant in Broward County, Florida who was assigned a COMPAS risk score between 2013-2014 and for whom two-year recidivism outcomes are available.

**Number of instances:** ~11,000 raw records; ~6,000 after ProPublica filtering

**Missing data:** Some fields (e.g., days_b_screening_arrest) have missing values. Instances with invalid or missing key fields are excluded per ProPublica methodology.

### Fields

**Key features:**
- `id`: Unique identifier
- `name`, `first`, `last`: Defendant name (should be excluded for privacy)
- `sex`: Male, Female
- `age`, `age_cat`: Age in years and category (<25, 25-45, >45)
- `race`: African-American, Asian, Caucasian, Hispanic, Native American, Other
- `juv_fel_count`, `juv_misd_count`, `juv_other_count`: Juvenile offense counts
- `priors_count`: Number of prior offenses
- `c_charge_degree`: Charge degree (F=Felony, M=Misdemeanor)
- `c_charge_desc`: Charge description
- `decile_score`, `score_text`: COMPAS risk score (1-10) and text label (Low, Medium, High)

**Target variables:**
- `two_year_recid`: Binary indicator of any recidivism within two years
- `two_year_violent_recid`: Binary indicator of violent recidivism within two years
- `is_recid`, `is_violent_recid`: General recidivism indicators

**Temporal fields:**
- `screening_date`: Date of COMPAS screening
- `c_jail_in`, `c_jail_out`: Jail entry/exit dates
- `c_offense_date`, `c_arrest_date`: Offense/arrest dates
- `compas_screening_date`: COMPAS assessment date

**Does the dataset contain data that might be considered confidential?**
Yes. The dataset includes personally identifiable information (names) and criminal history. ProPublica released this as public records data, but use should respect privacy considerations.

### Sensitive Information

**Sensitive attributes:** Race, sex, age

**Potential for discrimination:** This dataset has been central to debates on algorithmic fairness. Predictive models trained on this data may perpetuate racial disparities if not carefully evaluated and mitigated.

## Collection

### Data Acquisition

**How was data collected?**
- **COMPAS scores:** Generated by Northpointe's proprietary algorithm based on defendant questionnaire and criminal history
- **Recidivism outcomes:** Tracked via criminal records in Broward County over two years following COMPAS screening
- **Public records requests:** ProPublica obtained data via Freedom of Information Act requests to Broward County

**Who was involved?** Broward County criminal justice system (police, courts, jails)

**Timeframe:** Defendants screened 2013-2014; outcomes tracked through 2016

### Sampling Strategy

**Is the dataset a sample?** Yes, the dataset includes only defendants in Broward County, Florida who were assigned COMPAS scores and for whom two-year outcomes are available.

**Potential biases:**
- **Geographic:** Limited to one Florida county; may not generalize to other jurisdictions
- **Temporal:** Crime patterns and policing practices may have changed since 2013-2014
- **Selection:** Only includes individuals who entered the criminal justice system and received COMPAS assessments
- **Missing data:** Some defendants excluded due to incomplete records

## Preprocessing

### Filtering

ProPublica applied the following filters:
1. Days between screening and arrest within [-30, 30] days
2. Valid recidivism indicator (`is_recid != -1`)
3. Charge degree is F (felony) or M (misdemeanor)
4. COMPAS score is not "N/A"

These filters reduce the dataset from ~11,000 to ~6,000 instances.

### Transformations

- Categorical encoding: `age_cat`, `sex`, `race`, `c_charge_degree`, `score_text` are treated as categorical
- Temporal calculations: `days_b_screening_arrest` computed from screening and arrest dates

## Uses

### Intended Uses

- **Research:** Understanding algorithmic fairness, bias in risk assessment, recidivism prediction
- **Benchmarking:** Standard dataset for evaluating fairness-aware ML methods
- **Education:** Teaching ML ethics and fairness concepts

### Inappropriate Uses

- **Production deployment:** This dataset should NOT be used to build production risk assessment tools without extensive validation and stakeholder engagement
- **Discriminatory profiling:** Models should not be used to justify racial profiling or discriminatory policing
- **Automated decision-making:** Risk scores should not replace human judgment in high-stakes decisions (bail, sentencing)

## Ethical Considerations

### Risks

1. **Perpetuating bias:** Models trained on this data may inherit biases from historical policing and adjudication practices
2. **Privacy:** Dataset includes names and criminal records; re-identification is possible
3. **Stigmatization:** Publicizing recidivism predictions may harm individuals' reputations and reintegration
4. **Misuse:** Risk scores may be misinterpreted or used inappropriately by non-experts

### Mitigation

- **Anonymization:** Remove names and identifiers for research use
- **Fairness evaluation:** Always assess model performance across racial and gender groups
- **Transparency:** Clearly document limitations and potential biases
- **Stakeholder engagement:** Consult with affected communities and criminal justice professionals
- **Human oversight:** Models should inform, not replace, human decision-making

## Limitations

1. **Geographic specificity:** Data from one Florida county; may not generalize
2. **Temporal drift:** Crime patterns and policies may have changed since 2013-2014
3. **Recidivism definition:** Two-year window may miss longer-term patterns; definition of "recidivism" (re-arrest, not conviction) is contested
4. **Missing counterfactuals:** We observe outcomes only for individuals who entered the system
5. **Proprietary scores:** COMPAS scores are black-box predictions; original features and model are not public
